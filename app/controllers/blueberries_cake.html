Blueberries Cake

Blueberries Cake

4.9 from 25 reviews
  • Prep time: 10 min
  • Cook time: 10 min
  • Yield: 5
Save Recipe
Ingredients
For the Cake:
  • 1 ½ cups (190g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup (115g) unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup (150g) granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ cup (120ml) buttermilk
  • 1 ½ cups (225g) fresh or frozen blueberries (do not thaw if frozen)
For the Topping:
  • ¼ cup (50g) granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
Nutrition Facts
Serves 8
  • Total Fat : 10.4g
  • Cholesterol : 6%
  • Chalories: 250 g

Description

This Blueberries Cake is a moist, flavorful dessert bursting with fresh blueberries in every bite. The cake is perfect for tea-time treats, family gatherings, or simply indulging your sweet tooth!

Instructions

  • 01. Preheat the Oven: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and lightly flour a 9-inch round cake pan or line it with parchment paper. Prepare the Dry Ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Set aside. Cream Butter and Sugar: In a large bowl, use a hand or stand mixer to cream the softened butter and sugar until light and fluffy (about 3-4 minutes).
  • 02. Add Wet Ingredients: Beat in the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla extract. Scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed. Combine Wet and Dry Ingredients: Alternately add the dry ingredients and buttermilk to the wet mixture, starting and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix just until combined—do not overmix. Fold in Blueberries: Gently fold in the blueberries using a spatula, being careful not to crush them.
  • 03. Pour Batter into Pan: Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan and smooth the top with a spatula. Prepare the Topping: In a small bowl, mix the sugar and cinnamon. Sprinkle evenly over the top of the cake batter. Bake: Bake for 40-45 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center of the cake comes out clean. Cool: Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer it to a wire rack to cool completely.

Notes

i

Fresh vs. Frozen Blueberries: If using frozen blueberries, do not thaw them before adding them to the batter to prevent excess moisture. Buttermilk Substitute: If you don’t have buttermilk, make your own by mixing ½ cup of milk with 1 tablespoon of lemon juice or vinegar. Let it sit for 5 minutes before using.

i

Storage: Store the cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days or in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. Optional Glaze: Drizzle with a lemon glaze for added flavor. Mix 1 cup powdered sugar with 2 tablespoons of lemon juice and drizzle over the cooled cake.
